Majazz H. Allah

Hi! I am a recent graduate of Kennesaw State University (KSU), having earned my master's degree in Intelligent Robotic Systems, where my studies focused on a mixture of engineering / math education research, AI, computer vision, and roboethics. For the past 13 years, I have worked in the Atlanta area to empower students with STEM skills and knowledge, and to help them pursue careers in STEM fields.

Currently, I am a Co-Researcher & College Math Literacy Worker with the Accessible Calculus Project @ KSU . For the past 3 years I have worked with students, teachers, researchers, and faculty to improve the accessibility of college-level mathematics for students across collegiate and pre-collegiate levels, with a focus on elementary students in informal (community) learning environments. I am also the fonder and current Program Director of ATOM Robotics, where I mentor and teach 7th - 12th grade students in robotics.

Research Interests

I am interested in sensors & perception, robot design, and artificial intelligence -- and how these technologies can be postively applied towards autonomous exploration/conservation efforts, developing more harmonious interactions with people/the environment, as well as the impact robots have on society from a technological, ethical, and social perspective. I would like to work in the field of robotics engineering or space technology, and to continue to mentor students in STEM fields, especially those from underrepresented communities.

I am currently looking for a position full-time role or PhD program in robotics, ML, or computer vision. I am also open to research collaborations and consulting opportunities related to robotics education. I aspire to contribute to the field of robotics from a educational, technological, and policy perspective, and to help make technology more accessible, equitable and community centered.
