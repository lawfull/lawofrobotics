To Students: To any student reading this, I want you to know that you can do anything. I come from a slightly unconventional background into engingeering. I started at community college as a homeschooled highschooler, had to continue there because I didn't get into any universities graduating from HS, and had no real guidance towards the engineering world (let alone academia) besides sheer determination, curiosity, and a mom that helped me start pushing my boulder uphill. And now, I have two (and hopefully soon to be three) university degrees. You can do anything you set your mind to. dream big .

Selected Awards & Recognitions

  • 2025 Johnson STEM Activity Center - STEM Inspiration Award
    Awarded to two JSAC students (current or alumni) who have demonstrated a passion for STEM and have made significant contributions to the field of STEM or Engineering Education through their work, research, and/or community involvement.


  • 2025 Black In Robotics (BIR) + 3M MS Fellowship
    Awarded to 3 students in the US for their studies in a robotics related Master's program (such as Robotics, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Aeronautics, etc.) and their contributions to the field of robotics.
